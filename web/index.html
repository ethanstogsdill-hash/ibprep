📋 How to find contacts on LinkedIn:
  1. A LinkedIn search will open in a new tab
  2. Browse the results and find people you want to contact
  3. For each person, copy their: Name, Title, Location
  4. Go to the "Add Contact" tab and paste their info
  5. Or create a CSV and bulk import!

Tip: Use LinkedIn's filters to narrow results by seniority, location, etc.
